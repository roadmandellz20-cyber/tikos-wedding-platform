# Product guide

## What we are building

A polished, mobile-first wedding invitation and guest-page service for couples and families in The Gambia and the West African diaspora. It should make the invitation feel personal and premium while making the details and guest response easy to find and use.

The brand lockup is **YESLY by TIKOS**. YESLY is the lead brand; TIKOS names the wedding invitation and guest-page offer.

## Who it serves

- Couples planning a wedding in The Gambia or from the diaspora.
- Families and committees helping coordinate invitations and guest information.
- Guests who need to open the invitation, understand the event, and respond on a phone with minimal friction.

## Launch release: Celebrate

The launch page may present:

- TIKOS wedding websites / guest pages.
- Digital invitation design and printed invitations.
- RSVP and WhatsApp link sharing.
- Premium add-ons such as additional languages, custom domains, and livestream, clearly scoped as optional.
- Yesly Events as a small, separate planning pilot, with no implication that full-service planning is generally available.

Show how a guest page works. A visual preview must say when sample details or interactions are illustrative. Do not claim an RSVP has been saved unless a real connected service did so.

## Later releases — do not advertise as live at launch

- Plan: vendor directory, cost-per-guest tools, checklist, committee roles.
- Book: quotes, availability, bookings, deposits/mobile money, reviews, planner tools.
- Prepare: marriage registration routing, health bookings, counseling, and faith study. These require signed partner agreements and strong consent/privacy safeguards. The platform does not issue legal certificates or medical results. Sensitive test results stay with the facility and couple.

## Business model from the supplied one-page plan

TIKOS may earn through wedding-page and invitation design, printed invitation work, branding, and optional premium add-ons. Yesly Events may charge planning fees during its small pilot. Disclose commissions if vendor referrals are introduced. The supplied plan does not give public prices; do not make them up. Explain scope and show a quote before paid work starts.

## Experience principles

1. Lead with the invitation and the guest experience; keep the launch promise clear.
2. Make the primary path obvious: understand the offer → see a guest-page sample → understand how to get a design.
3. Put event facts and RSVP where guests can find them quickly on mobile.
4. Keep pages light for limited data connections and support local-language expansion without claiming translations that are not ready.
5. Use warm, joyful storytelling without making the interface ornate or hard to read.
6. Distinguish available features from samples, pilots, and future roadmap.
