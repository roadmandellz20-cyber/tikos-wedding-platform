details.

Can I share the wedding page on WhatsApp?

Yes. Your wedding page is designed to be easy to share as a link, including in WhatsApp messages.

Can I have printed invitations too?

Yes. Print choices and quantities can be discussed alongside your invitation design.

Can the page include another language?

Additional language options can be scoped with TIKOS as part of your design.

Does TIKOS include full wedding planning?

Wedding invitations and guest pages are the launch focus. Yesly Events is testing a small, separate planning pilot.
